Halloween family thrill coaster

TeamGeorgia Tech Theme Park Engineering Design
ContextFreshman Welcome Challenge
RoleTeam lead, four-person team
DatesSeptember 2026

A concept design for a family thrill coaster in a Halloween-themed land, proposed for a fictional developer's park. I led a four-person team through concept, design and pitch for the Freshman Welcome Challenge.

We designed multiple ride elements in SolidWorks, including track and cart, and built out the parts of a proposal that are easy to skip in a student project: physical schematics, safety guidelines and an actual business case. I also wrote the ride's storyline, which riders follow from queue to brake run.

It is the least conventionally technical thing on this site and it taught me the most about pitching. A ride concept has to survive both a structural review and a room of people deciding whether anyone wants to queue for it.
