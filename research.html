Beyond the Scan: how machine learning will advance brain tumor detection

Benchmarked five ensemble architectures on rare brain tumor MRI classification as the training set shrank toward a realistic low-data scenario, and identified a practical data-sufficiency threshold of roughly 28 training images. DenseNet169-LIME-TumorNet held up best.
