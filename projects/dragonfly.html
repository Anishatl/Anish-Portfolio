Project Dragonfly — liquid fuel lander

TeamGeorgia Tech Propulsive Landers
RoleAvionics team member
DatesSeptember 2026 – present

Propulsive Landers designs, builds and tests hybrid propulsive landers. I joined the avionics team in September 2026 and work on Dragonfly, a liquid fuel lander with a 2000 N thrust profile being built to a compressed three-month schedule.

Avionics

I support the onboard navigation board and the embedded control hardware for the team's hybrid lander — the first time I have worked on flight hardware rather than a robot, and the reason I am concentrating in computer hardware and emerging architectures.

Data acquisition

Separately from the flight hardware, I worked with the team to rebuild how we capture test data: a portable data analysis system, and a physical data collection system we can run on site during a test rather than reconstructing what happened afterwards.

Across the build

Dragonfly is a rapid sprint, so the lines between subteams are thin. I contribute across avionics, propulsion and structural design as one of the project's builders as we prepare to compete with the liquid lander.

This project is in progress. I will write it up properly once we have flown it.
