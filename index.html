I am a Computer Engineering student at Georgia Tech, concentrating in computer hardware and in robotics and autonomous systems. Four years with FIRST Robotics Team 1771 took me from CAD support to leading the design and build of our 2026 robot, where I drew the full electrical architecture, wired it, and manufactured most of its parts in-house.

My earliest engineering memory is my dad handing me a screwdriver in preschool to fix a TV remote. The habit stuck: find the real problem first, then learn whatever it takes to solve it. That is how a bowling injury turned into a pose-estimation app, and how watching my uncle drink turbid well water turned into a $2.41 filter. I am now on the avionics team for Georgia Tech Propulsive Landers, working on embedded control hardware for a liquid fuel lander.
