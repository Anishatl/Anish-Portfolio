Battery Crimp Resistance Testing

FRC applied research · Team 1771

MULTIMETER TESTING3 CONNECTION METHODSTEAM POWER RELIABILITY

Our robot's 12V 18Ah batteries — the only battery type allowed on FRC robots — were dying quickly mid-competition, and we suspected the quality of the lead crimps was the culprit, since poor crimps increase electrical resistance and waste power.

Method

I led the testing of three connection types: SB120 crimps alone, soldered leads alone, and a combined SB120-plus-solder approach. For each, I assembled the connections myself and measured starting and ending resistance with a multimeter, holding each reading for 20 seconds for consistency, with three repeated trials per method to reduce experimental error.

Outcome

The results gave our team clear, data-backed guidance on which connection method to standardize on for competition-day power delivery, directly improving the reliability of our robot's electrical system during matches.
