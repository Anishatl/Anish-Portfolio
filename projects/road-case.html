Robotics Team Road Case

FIRST Robotics Competition · Team 1771 · designed in SolidWorks

79″ × 36″ × 24″~450 LBS LOADEDDESIGNED, PARTS ORDERED

Team 1771 needed a single road case to transport an enormous amount of competition gear: 16 laptops, 4 mobile hotspots, 2 power packs, a 3D printer, a full sound system, 16 bleacher chairs, and assorted wiring and binders — all within a 79″ × 36″ × 24″ footprint.

Structural design

Anticipating a loaded weight of roughly 450 lbs, I selected four locking caster wheels rated for at least 250 lbs each, and after comparing several hinge and latch options, settled on medium clasp latches with 90° catchplates and full-height piano hinges for durability. Shelving mixes 0.125″–0.25″ plywood with 1″×1″ square dowel framing.

Power distribution

Because each power bank draws more than 15 amps, the case needed three independent circuits: one per power bank, plus one shared circuit for the 3D printer, laptops, and hotspots, routed through a 19-socket power distribution unit and weather-resistant cable glands so cords can exit safely from the case's sides.

The full design is complete in SolidWorks and parts have been ordered; assembly is planned using rivets and Titebond for speed without sacrificing durability, finished with a weatherproof spray coat.
