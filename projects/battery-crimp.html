Battery crimp resistance testing

TeamFIRST Robotics, Team 1771
TypeApplied research
Dates2025

Our 12V 18Ah competition batteries were dying faster than they should. Rather than replace them, I tested whether the connection itself was the problem.

3
Connection methods compared
3
Trials per method

I compared three approaches — SB120 crimps alone, soldered leads alone, and both together — measuring resistance across each with a multimeter over three trials per method. Connection resistance turns into heat and voltage sag under the current an FRC drivetrain pulls, so a poor crimp shows up as a battery that looks dead long before it is.

It is a small experiment, but it is the kind that decides whether you spend a season replacing batteries or fixing the thing that was killing them.
