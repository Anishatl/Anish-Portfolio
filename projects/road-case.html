Robotics team road case

TeamFIRST Robotics, Team 1771
Dates2025

A 79 × 36 × 24 inch road case designed in SolidWorks to move an entire team's gear and keep it usable while packed.

450 lb
Loaded weight
3
Separate power circuits

The case carries 16 laptops, four mobile hotspots, two power banks, a 3D printer and a full sound system, all charging and usable without unpacking. The electrical planning was the real constraint: each power bank draws over 15 A, so everything could not share one outlet, and the case runs three separate circuits to keep within what a venue will actually give you.
