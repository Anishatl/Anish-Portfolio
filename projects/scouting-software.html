Team 1771 Scouting & Strategy Software

FIRST Robotics Competition · Team 1771 · Strategy Lead, 2023–present

100,000+ DATA POINTSGOOGLE SHEETS + JOTFORM80+ SCOUTS TRAINED

As Strategy Lead for Team 1771, I designed and managed our entire data collection and analysis system across district events, state championships, and the World Championship — processing well over 100,000 individual data points a season, as well as driving the robot in matches and making strategy calls live to help our alliance win.

Data collection

I built seven Jotform-based data collection tools for the 2024 season (three qualitative, four quantitative), eight for 2025, and eleven for 2026, then trained more than 80 team members to run them live at competition. All submissions fed into a Google Sheets backend, split into a raw data layer and a processed data layer for security and consistency.

Analysis tools

On top of the raw data, I built a set of analysis tools including a Head-to-Head match predictor that outperformed the standardized FRC match predictor used globally, a Top 75 ranking tool that ranked every team in our division by success rate, IQR, and maximums, and Auton by Match/Team tools that pulled autonomous routine data by driver station and team.

This system directly informed alliance selection and match strategy at every event we attended from 2023 onward, including our 3-peat state championship from 2024-2026 and our Curie Division championship at the 2025 World Championship.

Education

In addition to creating tools to progress at competitions, I also started and led our team's strategy education department, teaching over 200 students across three years about what FIRST Robotics is, match strategy and game theory basics, advanced techniques to navigate competition rankings, data analytical methods, and how the hardware and software of our robot is directly driven by game theory decisions we make. In addition, I led multiple mentoring sessions of our elementary and middle school robotics teams, help robot showcases at county meetings and local science fairs, and had STEM nights for children in the community.
