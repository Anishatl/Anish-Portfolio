Custom 3D-Printed Beyblade

Personal engineering project

ONSHAPE CADENDER 3 PROBEAT REAL BEYBLADES IN TESTING

I wanted to see if I could design and 3D-print a Beyblade, launcher, and ripcord from scratch that actually outperformed the real ones — so I took real Beyblades and launchers apart to measure and understand exactly how they worked.

I redesigned the Beyblade for better aerodynamics and longer spin speeds in OnShape, then spent about a week adjusting my Ender 3 Pro's print and slicer settings to hit the tight tolerances needed for all the small interlocking parts to fit together properly.

In final testing, the 3D-printed Beyblade beat real, store-bought Beyblades every time, thanks to a higher infill density and a more impact-resistant design.
