Lithophane light boxes

TypePersonal project
Dates2024

Backlit nightlights with a lithophane photo panel, designed in CAD and given away as gifts. I have made dozens since, customised for specific people.

Two things decided whether these worked. The foot angle sets how light meets the panel — wrong angle and the image washes out or goes dark at the edges — and infill density sets the weight balance, which is what stops a tall thin object tipping off a nightstand. Both took more iterations than the photo panel itself.
